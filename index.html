Why the selvedge is the signature

On a real bolt of handloom cloth, the coloured stripe running down the edge — the selvedge — is the weaver's signature: it names the loom, the collective, sometimes the weaver by hand. We've borrowed that stripe for this page, because nothing here is generic stock; every piece is tied to the loom that made it, and every order supports the weaver directly.
